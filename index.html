SYNOPSIS & INFO

The Story

Gru and Lucy are fired from the Anti-Villain League after failing to capture Balthazar Bratt, a former child star turned villain who wants revenge on Hollywood. Because of this, the Minions go on strike and leave to find a new "evil boss."

Meanwhile, Gru discovers he has a long-lost twin brother named Dru, who invites him to the fictional country of Freedonia. There, Gru learns about his family history, while Agnes searches for a unicorn. In the end, Gru must unite with his family and the Minions to stop Bratt's plan.
